You were treating tight stop and small loss as the same thing. They are not. A 3% stop on a name that ranges 8.8% intraday is not protection — it is a coin flip you keep losing. Sizing by risk resolves it: a wider stop mechanically produces a smaller position, so the dollar loss stays capped either way. Room to breathe and bounded pain, not one or the other.

Worst case with every stop firing is 7.4% of book — deliberately inside the −15% warn rung, so the stop system can never by itself trigger the de-risking ladder.
