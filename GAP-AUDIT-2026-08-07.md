### The three findings worth carrying forward

**1. G41 changed a number that had already been reported.** The 2026-08-03 briefing stated
`+$261.77` of residual "market move". Corrected, that run's real market performance was
**−$1,021.52** — a $1,283 swing and a sign flip. Cash deployment had been narrated as performance.
Any conclusion drawn from that day's attribution should be re-read with this in mind.

**2. G44's earlier write-up was itself wrong, and the correction is the lesson.** It had been
recorded as a "likely misread TTM/annualized figure — a data error." It was not:

- smith-quality's arithmetic was **correct and exactly reproducible** — `413.9/50.8 = 8.15x`, then
  `350.1/256.1 = 1.37x`. Its 8.1x → 1.4x was right.
- The $256.1M was **real money** — pretax income fell to $83.3M from $381.6M while operating income
  barely moved, so ~$267M of below-the-line expense genuinely landed.
- What it actually was: MRVL closed **Celestial AI + XConn** that quarter (+$2.8B goodwill, +$1.0B
  intangibles) alongside a $1.0B 5.300% 2036 notes issue. At 5.3% on $1B, incremental run-rate
  interest is ~$13M/quarter — nowhere near $200M. The 10-Q's own interest expense line is **$52.8M**
  (+8.4% YoY), i.e. ~6.6x coverage. Healthy.
- So the failure was **interpretation, not calculation**: a one-time acquisition-financing charge
  that yfinance buckets under "Interest Expense" was read as a permanent change in cost of debt —
  and the strategist's MRVL veto and trim rested on it.

Prevention is now in `smith-quality.md`: a >2x single-quarter coverage move is a data question
before a credit finding. Also documented there — **`get_financials` silently ignores
`period="quarterly"` and returns ANNUAL columns**; the honoured parameter is `frequency`. Nothing in
the response indicates which basis you got.

**3. G19's plan-block had been hiding a real concentration.** DRAM is one line in cluster/drift math,
so its constituents are invisible. Look-through on the 2026-08-07 book:

| Samsung | **0.00%** | 0.78% | +0.78pp |
| Sandisk | 3.71% | 3.94% | +0.23pp |

The book holds Samsung with no direct Samsung position, and ~$352 (1.03% of equity) of what counts
as equity is actually a T-bill inside DRAM. Stored at `data_cache.look_through_2026_08_07`.
