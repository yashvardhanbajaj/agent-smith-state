## 3. Single-Factor Risk Verdict

This is, at 99.278%, no longer "AI-capex-heavy" -- it is functionally a single-factor book. Every cluster except Enterprise Software (NOW, 0.72%) is some node on the same chain: chips get made (AI Semis/Fabs), stored (AI Memory/Storage), moved (AI Networking/Optics), powered/cooled (AI Power/Cooling, now down to one name), and consumed/monetized (Compute/Hyperscaler + OEM). Cash is 0.05%, itself a band breach, so it isn't a real ballast either. The one nominally "different" name, NOW, is itself pitched as an AI-agent monetization story, so even that slice isn't truly uncorrelated to the same capex narrative -- it's just a different point in the value chain (software layer vs infrastructure). Practically: there is no genuinely uncorrelated slice left in this book. A hyperscaler capex pause, a memory-pricing air-pocket, or a broad "AI monetization doubt" repricing (the kind already visible in MRVL's 07-16 capex-fear selloff and ORCL's recurring debt/FCF worries) would hit essentially the whole portfolio at once, not a diversifiable corner of it. The AI Power/Cooling collapse to 3.58% (from a 15% target) and AI Memory/Storage's breach to 27.9% (concentrated further by SNDK's cap breach) both point the same direction: the book has been drifting from "AI-thematic with balance across sub-cycles" to "AI-thematic concentrated in memory + fabs," which is a narrower, more correlated bet than the policy intends.
