- **CXMT/DUV structural threat (theme 1) is UNCHANGED by any of today's news.** Nothing found today addresses China's domestic lithography/memory buildout; today's catalysts are entirely financing-sentiment and single-earnings-print driven.

## Bottom line for the desk

The single named fundamental anchor (MSFT capex beat) is genuinely structural-positive for theme 3/4.
