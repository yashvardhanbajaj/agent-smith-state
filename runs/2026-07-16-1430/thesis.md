## 3. Single-factor risk verdict

This book is effectively one bet dressed as 28 positions: ~96% sits somewhere in the AI-capex chain (memory → semicap → fabs/IP → optics/interconnect → custom silicon/GPUs → hyperscaler/neocloud demand → power-infra), and today's session shows exactly why that matters — nearly every name moved down together, in excess of SMH's own -2%, on no company-specific news. The only genuinely uncorrelated slice is NOW + CQQQ at ~4% of the book — and even NOW's SaaS thesis carries an AI-disruption tail risk (agents cannibalizing seat-based software) that could make it move *with* an AI story turning sour rather than against it, while CQQQ's China-tech names (PDD/Meituan/Tencent/Baidu) are a genuinely different macro driver (China consumer/regulatory, not US AI capex) but are themselves a concentrated single-country bet. There is no true hedge sleeve in this book today.
