## Cluster thesis — strengthening / early
The 800G→1.6T transition has barely started and its bottleneck is a physical input (InP/EML) that is short into 2029 — a supply-constrained
transition is early, not late, however far the prices have run. FALSIFIER: two consecutive quarters of module-maker gross margin compression while
revenue grows, or a credible report that EML supply/demand has crossed into balance before end-2027.

## Margin pool
TOWARD: InP/EML light-source supply and vertically integrated laser capacity (LITE, COHR) + scale-up silicon content (ALAB, MRVL DSP).
AWAY FROM: pluggable module assembly and copper connector / cable assembly (APH, and the assembly half of any module maker).
Mechanism: when the scarce input is the laser, the assembly step is a pass-through; CPO reinforces this by deleting the pluggable while
*increasing* external-laser demand.
