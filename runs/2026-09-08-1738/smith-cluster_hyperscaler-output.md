## The cluster splits in two, and the split is the whole analysis
MSFT/GOOG and NBIS/IREN are not four expressions of one tailwind. The hyperscalers spend their own
operating cash flow; the neoclouds rent capital to buy GPUs and re-let them. With the 10-yr +12.4bp
over a month, CPI on 09-11 and FOMC 09-15/16, cost of capital is the transmission channel — and it
touches only half of this cluster. Ranking them on one axis set is the error to avoid.

## Margin pool
Toward: contracted-offtake neoclouds with named IG counterparties, and in-house-silicon owners.
Away from: speculative-capacity neoclouds funded by converts and GPU collateral. CRWV Q2 2026 net
interest expense $640M vs $267M — interest +140% against revenue +112%. When financing cost
outgrows revenue, the margin pool migrates from the renter to the lender and to the vendor
(NVDA taking equity/vendor positions rather than pure sales).

## Redundancy
