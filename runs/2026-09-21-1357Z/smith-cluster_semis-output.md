## 3. Margin pool
**Toward:** advanced packaging and test — CoWoS-class capacity (TSM), inspection/metrology
steps per wafer (KLAC), test seconds per die (TER). The incremental AI dollar buys packaging
and test capacity, not another scanner.
**Away from:** mature-node front-end wafer tools, and litho *unit* count — EUV ASP rises but
unit growth is gated by High-NA adoption pace, so ASML's share of the incremental dollar falls
even with the moat fully intact. Also away from fabless silicon *pricing* power: AMD's
+107% YoY datacenter growth and Nebius's +25% EPYC rate hike are demand proof that the
bottleneck is binding upstream, which is where the rent accrues.
